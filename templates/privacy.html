Contact the Data Protection Officer

The Data Protection Officer handles privacy questions, requests, and complaints for the city. Write to {{ details.email }}, call {{ details.phone }}, or send a letter to {{ details.address }}. Please ask for the Data Protection Officer and say what you want us to do.
