How we use your message. We use your name, email address, optional phone number, and message only to reply and keep a record of your request. Messages are kept for two years, then deleted securely, unless a records law requires longer.

You can ask to see, correct, or erase your details at any time, or withdraw your consent. The privacy policy explains your rights under the Data Privacy Act and how to reach the Data Protection Officer.

When you will hear back

The City Information Office replies within two working days. Questions about permits and documents may take longer because the answer comes from the office that handles them.
